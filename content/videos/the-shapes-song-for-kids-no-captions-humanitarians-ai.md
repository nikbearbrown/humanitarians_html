## The lyrics do the teaching

The song does not just name each shape, it describes what makes it that shape. A circle is round like the sun. A square has four sides. A triangle has three corners. That small addition, a defining feature attached to every name, is what turns the song from a list into a lesson. A child who can sing "a square has four sides" has learned something a flashcard alone would not have taught as memorably.
