## What meta tags actually control

Meta tags are the instructions you drop alongside your lyrics that tell the AI music model how to build the track, not just what to say. Rather than describing structure, mood, and arrangement in a separate prompt, meta tags let you encode that control directly into the song itself. The song frames this as the difference between a track that comes out flat and one that has real shape and intention behind it.

## Structure, mood, and intensity

The categories this track walks through map to the practical decisions every AI music creator faces. There are tags for structure and flow, controlling how a song moves from section to section. There are tags for mood and tone, letting you signal when a moment should feel triumphant, tense, or quiet. And there are tags for intensity, the difference between a passage that should sit back and one that should be shouted. Getting these right is what separates a track that sounds arranged from one that sounds like it was generated and left alone.

## Instrumentation and sound design

Beyond structure and mood, meta tags also reach into the sonic details: cueing an instrumental solo, setting a specific atmosphere, or bringing in a sound effect to punctuate a moment.
