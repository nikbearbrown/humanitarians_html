Yatra presents a weekly recap from Humanitarians AI built around testing one AI tool at a time using a fixed five-stage method. This week's tool was Gordy, and the video reports exactly how far the week's work got.

## The five-stage method: pick, use, make, write, publish

The series runs on a deliberately fixed structure: pick one tool, use it for real, make something with it, write it up, and publish. Yatra treats this as a status board rather than a simple checklist, and is explicit that a given week can end mid-pipeline, with some stages closed and others still open.

## What Gordy is, in one line

Gordy's own tool page describes it as a two-mode music artist brand strategist and sonic identity architect, built for independent artists, music teams, and what the page calls the Musinique era. Yatra treats that one-line description as the entire public account of the tool, which is exactly why the rest of the video focuses on what using it actually produced.

## This week's status: four stages closed, one pending
