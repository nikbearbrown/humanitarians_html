## Six ways to say thank you

This particular video centers on gratitude, and it doesn't stop at a single phrase. It works through six distinct ways to express thanks in Hindi: a plain thank you (धन्यवाद, Dhanyavaad), saying you appreciate something (मैं इसकी सराहना करता हूँ, Main iski sarahna karta hoon), thanking someone for their help (आपकी मदद के लिए धन्यवाद, Aapki madad ke liye dhanyavaad), giving many thanks (आपका बहुत धन्यवाद, Aapka bahut dhanyavaad), expressing gratitude for a person (मैं आपके लिए आभारी हूँ, Main aapke liye aabhaari hoon), and thanking someone for their kindness (आपकी दया के लिए धन्यवाद, Aapki daya ke liye dhanyavaad). Covering six variations instead of one line reinforces that gratitude isn't a single fixed phrase in Hindi any more than it is in English, and gives a learner several real options depending on context.
