## An accidental find

## Why VEO 3 handles this so well

The qualities that make this work are the same ones that are hard to fake with practical toy photography or basic stop motion: convincing motion blur that sells speed and impact, cinematic depth of field that pulls focus onto the fighters, lighting that gives static plastic a sense of weight and life, and camera movement that tracks the fight rather than sitting locked off. Put together, those elements turn a simple toy setup into something that looks like a genuine fight scene rather than a photo of two figures posed on a table.
