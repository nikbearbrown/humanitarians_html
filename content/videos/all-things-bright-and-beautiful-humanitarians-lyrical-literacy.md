## Why a hymn becomes a brain workout

The Lyrical Literacy project treats music as active exercise for the brain rather than passive background sound. Singing engages memory, language processing, rhythm, and emotional response all at once, which is why a simple, repetitive song like this one can function as a genuine cognitive workout. The lyrics walk through recognizable, concrete images, flowers that open, birds that sing, glowing colors, tiny wings, purple mountains, rivers running by, which gives the brain a steady stream of sensory and linguistic material to track and anticipate as the melody repeats and builds across verses.
