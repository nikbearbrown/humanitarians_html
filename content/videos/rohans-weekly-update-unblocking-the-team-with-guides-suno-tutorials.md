Rohan gives a weekly update from Humanitarians AI focused on training material rather than a research result. Two problems were blocking the team that week, and this video covers what got built to fix both.

## Unblocking the marketing team's GitHub workflow

The marketing team could make things without any trouble. What they couldn't do was get finished work onto GitHub, and since the whole team is non-technical, that single step was stopping completed videos from ever being submitted. Rohan had already solved this workflow for himself, so at the start of the week he ran a live workshop walking the team through it, then packaged the same setup as a video. The result is that nobody has to sit in a live session to learn the process going forward.

## A three-part Suno tutorial series, rebuilt in code

Most of the week went into a Suno tutorial series: three parts, all finished, all in 4K, totaling about 10 and a half minutes of training material for new team members. Part one gets a new user to their first generated song. Part two covers prompt craft, how to describe a sound and actually get that sound back. Part three covers refining and exporting a finished track. A deliberate technical choice runs through all three parts: every screen of Suno's interface is rebuilt in code rather than screen recorded, so the tutorials won't go stale the next time Suno ships an interface redesign.

## Writing down what used to be tribal knowledge

The other piece of the week's work is written rather than filmed. A new fellow needs four separate accounts before they can make anything: Discord, Suno, Midjourney, and Adobe Creative Cloud. Right now, getting through that setup is tribal knowledge, something you find out only by asking someone who already went through it. Rohan is writing it down as proper documentation, with the explicit goal that "how do I get access" stops being a question anyone has to ask twice.

## What's next: the Midjourney series

Rohan previews the next piece of work: a Midjourney tutorial series using the same approach, interface rebuilt in code, written for people with no background in the tool. He expects it done by the end of the following week, with the signup guide landing alongside it.
