What does a large language model look like if you had to draw it? This short Midjourney animation test takes a literal swing at the question, turning Meta's LLaMA 4 into a physical character: half wool, half mech, rendered in a clean 3D minimal style and set loose in a piece of robotic surrealism.

## A model made of wool and metal

The design leans on the pun built into the model's own name. LLaMA 4 becomes an actual llama, but one whose fleece gives way to circuitry and armor plating. It's a simple visual trick, but it does real work: instead of illustrating a neural network with the usual glowing nodes and wireframes, the piece gives the abstraction a body, a texture, and a silhouette you can actually look at. The 3D minimal aesthetic keeps the shape legible even as it swings between organic and mechanical.

## Narration that doesn't explain, it performs

Rather than a voiceover that describes what a language model does, the test pairs the animation with Jamaican Patois spoken word. The narration reaches for logic, silence, and rhythm as ways of talking about how a model "thinks," treating the mind of the machine less like an engineering diagram and more like something with a pulse. That choice fits the surreal tone of the visuals: this isn't a product demo, it's a mood piece about artificial identity, using language and cadence most AI content never touches.
