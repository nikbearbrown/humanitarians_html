Writing a song from a blank page is hard even when you have musical training. This tutorial shows a different starting point: a built-in "ghost artist" profile inside the Lyrical Literacy tool that already carries a voice, a backstory, and a musical identity, so a user with no songwriting experience can generate a full, styled set of lyrics with a single short command.

## What a ghost artist is

The ghost artist demonstrated here is Champa Jaan, a fully built profile that shapes everything the tool generates once it's invoked. Her profile influences the lyrics, tone, and musical direction of any song generated under her name. Her voice is described as warm, soft, and slightly smoky with emotional depth, and her sound blends Bollywood jazz, lullaby song, and Hindustani classical ornamentation. The backstory situates her in the old Lucknow kotha music tradition, a world where poetry, storytelling, and classical music were deeply connected, and frames her as coming from the forgotten history of tawaif, courtesan-musicians who preserved music, poetry, and emotional storytelling. Her thematic core is remembrance and emotional truth, and what her heart speaks when she sings is love, memory, tenderness, and quiet grief. All of that shapes how the underlying AI writes lyrics once you invoke her.

## Generating lyrics with simple commands

The simplest way to use the profile is the command "random champa," which generates a full set of lyrics in the Champa style. The tool starts with a short vocal warm-up before generating the lyrics themselves, and it automatically selects the genre, background style, ornaments, and instruments based on the Champa profile without any further input needed.

From there, commands can get more specific. Typing "random lullaby champa" generates a full song connected to a lullaby theme while staying within the Champa style and story. The tutorial notes that almost anything can be layered into a Champa command: a place, an emotion, a memory, a character, a situation, or a season. The word "champa" itself keeps the artist's style locked in place while letting you add other ideas before it. For example, "folk champa" generates lyrics inspired by a folk tradition such as thumri, which tends to express love, longing, and emotional depth, while a command like "birthday champa" shifts the tempo and mood to something faster and more celebratory while still keeping Champa's character and storytelling style intact.

## Other commands and switching artists

Typing "list" surfaces the different commands available in the tool, useful for discovering other ways to shape a generation beyond the ones demonstrated directly. The tool also supports switching between different artist profiles depending on the style you're after, with Roselin mentioned as an Afrojazz-oriented option and Djit for Punjabi rap, giving users a way to pick an entirely different musical identity for a different project.

## From lyrics to a finished song in Suno

Once lyrics are ready, the next step is Suno, an AI platform that turns text into an actual produced song. The lyrics generated in Lyrical Literacy are pasted directly into Suno's lyrics section, and then style prompts are added on top: details like whether the voice should be male or female, a soft or high pitch, background style, and which instruments to use. A title is added, and clicking create generates two versions of the song, often differing in tempo or pitch. If neither version is quite right, the style section can be edited and the song regenerated. One practical constraint worth noting: the style prompt has to stay within a fixed character limit. Once a version is found that works, it can be liked, pinned, and even turned into a persona, which makes it possible to reuse that same voice and tempo for future songs without rebuilding the style from scratch.
