## Nine colors, nine anchors

The song moves through red, blue, green, yellow, white, black, brown, purple, and orange in quick, tightly rhymed verses. Each color gets paired with something a young child has already seen and touched: red apples, blue sky, green grass, yellow stars, white snow, the black night sky, a big round brown bear, juicy purple grapes, and orange carrots and pumpkins. That pairing is the whole pedagogical strategy. A color word on its own is just a sound; a color word attached to a real object a child can picture becomes a piece of usable vocabulary.
