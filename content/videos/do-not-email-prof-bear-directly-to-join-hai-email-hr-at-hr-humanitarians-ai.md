## Why this video exists

He receives twenty to thirty emails a day from people asking basic questions about joining, and answering each one individually is not something his schedule allows. This video is his way of pointing everyone toward the resources that already answer those questions, rather than leaving people to guess where to look.

## Where to actually go

The organization maintains a "For HIFS" (Humanitarians AI Fellows) playlist packed with videos, with more added regularly, along with a GitHub repository where you can look at what Fellows are actually building. The Humanitarians AI website lays out ongoing projects, and the Fellows page includes contact information for anyone who wants to join. The process itself is straightforward: you email HR and get onboarding information directly from them, not from Professor Bear.

## What to check before you ask a question

Before emailing anyone, the video points to weekly reports published by current Fellows, which show concretely what people are expected to produce and how they document their work. Reviewing a few of these gives a much clearer picture of what joining actually involves than a one-off email ever could. The message is direct: nearly all of the questions people ask have already been answered somewhere in the existing videos, playlists, and reports. If a genuine gap turns up, HR can flag it and a new video can address it, but that process starts with HR, not with a direct message to Professor Bear.
