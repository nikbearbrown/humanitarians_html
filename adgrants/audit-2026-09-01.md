## 1. Why the account has never served an impression

What the account looks like today:

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Account type | Ad Grants (Billing says "We don't bill you") |
| Campaigns | 3 Search campaigns, all "Eligible": Humanitarians AI ($11/day, since Nov 6 2025), Fellows ($110.42/day), AI Literacy ($190/day), both since Dec 24 2025 |
| Bid strategy | Maximize conversions on all three, no target CPA |
| Conversion tracking | Account-default "Page view" conversion action flagged **Needs attention**; Engagement actions "Active"; zero conversions ever recorded |
| Keywords | 137, **all broad match**, all "Eligible" (e.g. "AI Trust", "Agentic AI", "Large Language Models", "prompt engineering guide") |
| Ads | 6 responsive search ads, Eligible, strength Good/Excellent |
| Targeting | United States only (Bear's intent: the US plus every OPT-eligible source country, since F-1/OPT students travel home and study remotely; the plan in adgrants/ defaults to the US plus the 24 largest F-1 source countries), Google Search only, all day, no device or schedule restrictions, audiences in Observation mode (not restricting) |
| Impressions, all time | 0 |

Nothing is disapproved, paused, or mis-targeted. The account is losing every auction. Three things stack up:

1. **Maximize conversions with no conversion history.** Smart Bidding sets the bid from predicted conversion probability. With zero conversions ever and a broken page-view action, it has nothing to predict from and bids at or near nothing. Google's own Ad Grants guidance says not to use Manual CPC, Maximize clicks or Target impression share because they cap bids at $2, but that advice assumes conversion tracking works. Here it does not.
2. **Head terms.** "Prompt engineering", "Large Language Models", "Agentic AI", "AI literacy course" are among the most expensive commercial AI keywords. Ad Grants ads are shown only in the positions paid advertisers leave behind, so on these terms a grant account effectively never enters the auction.
3. **Broad match** on those terms widens them further into paid-advertiser territory rather than narrowing them.

Your reading is right: on generic AI keywords an Ad Grant is unwinnable. The fix is not a bigger bid, it is thousands of specific queries nobody is paying for, each with a matching page.

### What to change in the account (in this order)

1. **Fix conversion tracking first.** Confirmed 2026-09-01 with Tag Assistant: the "Page view" action (ID AW-16992035158, label 1vkaCKWW1r0bENbCt6Y_) is "Tag inactive", last activity Aug 19 2026. The account has 7 actions: three GA4 imports (video_start / video_progress / video_complete), two "Website" actions that need the Google Ads tag (Page view, Donate), and two YouTube-hosted actions that only count from video ads. None has ever recorded a conversion because (a) the Google Ads tag was not on the site, (b) the GA4 video events never fire because the YouTube embeds lacked `enablejsapi=1`, and (c) with zero ad clicks there is nothing to attribute anyway. Fixed in this change: the AW tag is installed site-wide (`components/analytics/GoogleAdsTag.tsx`), the Page view conversion fires on video play (a real engagement, not every page load), the Donate page fires its conversion once `NEXT_PUBLIC_AW_LABEL_DONATE` is set from that action's Tag setup, and every YouTube embed now has `enablejsapi=1`. Previously: the site ran GA4 tag G-HQ685S9XPH and no Google Ads conversion tag. In Google Ads go to Goals > Conversions > Summary, open the "Page view" action that says Needs attention, and either repair the GA4 import or create Google Ads conversion actions for: Fellows application submit, donation click/complete, newsletter signup, video article "engaged view" (60s+ or scroll depth via GA4 event). Ad Grants wants conversions on meaningful actions, not raw page views.
2. **Get impressions flowing while the conversion data builds.** Switch the three existing campaigns to **Maximize clicks with a $2.00 max CPC bid limit** (allowed under Ad Grants). Once a campaign has ~15 conversions in 30 days, move it back to Maximize conversions.
3. **Change match type.** Pause the broad-match head terms. Re-add anything worth keeping as phrase match.
4. **Launch the long-tail campaigns** (section 3). The existing three campaigns can stay as the "brand/program" layer with a small budget.
5. Keep the Ad Grants hygiene rules: no single-word keywords, no generic keywords, pause keywords with Quality Score 1-2, at least 2 ad groups per campaign, at least 2 sitelinks, account CTR at or above 5%.

The compliance page at /compliance states "All campaigns use conversion-based bidding strategies" as a rule. That is a best-practice recommendation from Google, not a policy requirement. Maximize clicks with a $2 cap is compliant. Consider softening that line.
